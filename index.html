The problem is not bad earbuds. It is bad timing.

People who wear earbuds all day know the exact kind of frustration that only appears when the meeting starts, the train pulls out, or the last mile of a run begins.

1
They die at the worst possible moment. A call is in progress, your playlist is moving, and suddenly the battery warning becomes the main character.
2
There is no fast, graceful backup. Charging cases help later, not now. Swapping devices or waiting on a case is the opposite of instant relief.
3
Buying a second pair is a workaround, not a solution. A spare set is expensive, easy to forget, and still leaves you with a dead pair exactly when you need it.
4
Most charging options break the experience. To recharge, you usually have to stop listening, pocket the buds, and interrupt the thing you were doing.

How úPods works

A braided cable keeps it ready at all times. The magnetic cradles snap onto the earbuds and trickle-charge them while your phone keeps streaming audio.

01

Plug the USB-C end into your phone.

The cable starts drawing a tiny amount of power from the phone, with a design tuned for emergency top-ups instead of heavy drain.

02

Let the magnetic cradles find the earbuds.

Each branch ends in a compact magnetic dock that aligns with the bottom of the earbud stem in one smooth motion.

03

Trickle-charge through the contact points.

The cradle mirrors the charging-case contacts, giving the earbuds just enough power to recover without drama.

04

Keep listening without interruption.

Bluetooth playback stays active the entire time, so the music, call, or podcast keeps going while the battery climbs.

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people usually ask first.

Does it work with all wireless earbuds?

It is designed for AirPods-style earbuds with stem-mounted contact points. Broader compatibility would depend on how closely the earbud shape matches the contact geometry.

Will it stop the music while charging?

No. The core idea is zero interruption: Bluetooth audio stays connected while the cable trickle-charges the earbuds through the magnetic dock.

How much battery does it use from the phone?

The system is designed to consume under 1.5% of phone battery for an emergency charge session, so it behaves more like a rescue line than a full power bank.

Is this meant to replace the charging case?

No. It is an emergency, in-the-moment solution for keeping earbuds alive while you keep moving, talking, or listening.
